The latest episode of the podcast, feature hybrid cloud expert and Senior Manager at Dell, Kenny Lowe.

The Cloud Talking is a podcast aimed at getting you closer to members of the community, talking about their career journey, the community, and a guest topic. We start each episode by asking "What do you tell your non-techie friends and family what you do?".

Head over to [Anchor](https://anchor.fm/thecloudtalking/episodes/Hybrid-Cloud--Ecosystems-and-Toys-R-Us-with-Kenny-Lowe-e1e236s) to see the episode on your favourite podcast app or listen directly from my [blog](https://blog.m12d.com/the-cloud-talking/).

Kenny has worked in IT for the last fifteen years in a broad range of industries, predominantly focused on Microsoft solutions. He currently leads the global team responsible for technical evangelism for Microsoft hybrid cloud solutions at Dell Technologies, engaging with the community, partners, and customers to share knowledge and collect feedback to improve the hybrid Azure ecosystem.

You can find Kenny on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/kennylowe) and his blog [Azure Stack Tips](https://blueboxes.co.uk).
