Over the past few months I have been working with Ensono in the United States, looking to bring more cloud-native and DevOps focus to their consulting and advising services.

However, I finished my second spell at Ensono on the 16th March and today I start a new journey with [Transparity](https://www.transparity.com/). I join the leadership team as Head of DevOps.

Already an established Microsoft partner in modern workplace, I join Transparity to drive forward their offerings in Microsoft Azure, building new offerings and services for their new and existing customers.

Part of my work will include the responsibility for the cloud management platform which will be released later in 2021.

I am really looking forward to starting my journey with Transparity and cannot wait to showcase some of the things we are looking to do!
