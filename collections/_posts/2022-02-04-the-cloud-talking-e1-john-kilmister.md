Launching the new podcast which gets you closer to members of the cloud community is Software Architect and serverless enthusiast John Kilmister.

The Cloud Talking is a podcast aimed at getting you closer to members of the community, talking about their career journey, the community, and a guest topic. We start each episode by asking "What do you tell your non-techie friends and family what you do?".

Head over to [Anchor](https://anchor.fm/thecloudtalking/episodes/Serverless-and-Sandwich-Years-with-John-Kilmister-e1dp550) to see the episode on your favourite podcast app or listen directly from my [blog](https://blog.m12d.com/the-cloud-talking/).

John Kilmister is a Software engineer, blogger, and speaker that enjoys exploring new technologies to solve real-world problems.

A certified Azure Solutions Architect with a background in e-commerce companies he currently works as a software architect, leading teams working on Azure-based backend, web, and mobile applications.

You can find John on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/johnkilmister) and his blog [blueboxes.co.uk](https://blueboxes.co.uk)
